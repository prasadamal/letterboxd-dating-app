# Privacy Policy — ReelMates

**Effective date:** October 2, 2026  
**Contact:** support@reelmates.app

ReelMates (“we”) operates the ReelMates mobile app and related services. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

## Information we collect

- **Account data:** email, display name, age, city, bio, hobbies.
- **Taste data:** movie like/dislike/skip ratings and derived compatibility scores.
- **Messages:** text you send to other users through in-app chat.
- **Technical data:** basic logs needed to operate the service (errors, timestamps).

We do **not** sell personal data.

## How we use information

- Create and secure your account
- Show daily film picks and match you with compatible users
- Deliver messaging between matched users
- Improve reliability and prevent abuse

## Storage & processors

Data is stored in **Supabase (PostgreSQL)** on infrastructure you configure for your deployment. Passwords are stored as **bcrypt** hashes. Authentication uses signed JWTs.

## Retention

We retain account data until you delete your account or request deletion. Messages and ratings may be deleted with the account.

## Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion by emailing **support@reelmates.app**.

## Children

ReelMates is intended for users **18+**. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date will change when we do.

## International users

If you use ReelMates outside your home country, your data may be processed where our servers are hosted.
