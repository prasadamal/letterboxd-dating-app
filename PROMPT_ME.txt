1. THE IDEA
-----------
Bring film-loving people together, based only on movies. "Find your film people."
- Everyone gets the same 10 films every day (the "daily ten") and rates them:
  liked / disliked / haven't seen.
- From ratings you get a film personality (14 archetypes), film friends, a taste-match %,
  and later (opt-in) dating.
- The founder's view: "the algorithm is everything" — daily picks, matching and the challenge
  link must all be state of the art.
- Lead with the game and friends; dating is the surprise later, kept behind a launch gate.


2. THE END PRODUCT
------------------
A daily film game + film diary + taste-matching social app for India first (Malayalam, Tamil,
Hindi, Telugu cinema are an edge) with:
- a shareable daily result / personality card and challenge links that spread on Instagram,
  WhatsApp and Telegram;
- friends and taste comparison;
- dating that opens country by country when enough men and women join;
- later a Plus subscription (not built; no paywall until people return daily).


3. FEATURES THAT EXIST TODAY
----------------------------
- Daily ten (slot-based picker, server/lib/films/dailyPicker.js, docs/ALGORITHMS.md):
  10 slots a day — crowd favourite, new-ish, dark side, Indian cinema (rotating Malayalam,
  Hindi, South Indian, Marathi/Bengali), director's pick (rotating Nolan, Tarantino, Scorsese,
  Kubrick, Miyazaki, Bong, Lijo...), world cinema, feel-good, blockbuster, art house, hidden gem.
  One face-off day a week (Marvel vs DC, Nolan vs Villeneuve, Tarantino vs Scorsese, Ghibli vs
  Pixar, Lijo vs Dileesh...). Rules: no director/franchise twice a day, max 5 English films,
  spread of genres/languages/eras, 40-day rest before a film returns, prefer divisive films.
  Most films should be ones a film geek has seen (~7 of 10). Each card shows its slot label
  ("Hidden gem: most people haven't seen this one"). Deterministic per day (daily_sets table).
- Connection score v2 (server/lib/tasteMatch.js): agreement weighted by how surprising it is,
  uses "haven't seen", diary stars, favourites, a taste profile (genre/language/era/director/
  style), confidence-aware (one lucky film can't make 95%). Simulation: top-5 accuracy at
  7 days improved 59% -> 69%.
- Dating deck v2 (server/lib/deckRanking.js): two-sided ranking (you'd like them AND they'd
  like you back), Thompson sampling, recency, newcomer lift. Dating gate currently 50 men +
  50 women per country (marketing plan suggests ~500 each before promoting dating).
- Challenge link (/c/:code): rate today's 10, share a link anywhere; the friend rates the same
  10 in any browser with no account; identical answers = 100%; liked vs disliked is a real
  disagreement, seen vs not seen counts half; verdicts from "Film soulmates" to
  "Opposites attract?"; Open Graph previews; owner gets a notification.
- Film diary (log watched films with date, half-star rating, note, rewatch), watchlist,
  explore/search, collections, friend codes (random, rate-limited), personality card sharing,
  report/block, account deletion, admin page.
- Catalog: 436 films on main; PR #34 (OPEN, waiting for the founder to merge) adds 107 more
  (543 total: Tarantino, Nolan, Scorsese, Marvel/DC, Indian, French/world, latest, horror),
  Wikidata director/cast/runtime (server/data/wikidataFacts.json), and 22 real posters that are
  public-domain (Wikimedia Commons, server/data/posters.json). Modern posters are studio-owned:
  all other films use the typographic poster design. IMDb ids are stored for a future licensed
  source (TMDB terms must be checked before commercial use).

5. TECHNICAL MAP
----------------
- Server: Express + supabase-js (server/), deployed on Render free tier as reelmates-api-kshi
  (https://reelmates-api-kshi.onrender.com; sleeps after 15 min idle, ~15-60 s wake).
  Admin page: /admin on that host (unlock with ADMIN_API_KEY from Render's Environment tab).
- Database: Supabase project "moviematch" (free tier; keep-alive workflow). Migrations in
  supabase/migrations (e.g. 20261121120000_daily_sets_and_challenges — applied live).
- Mobile app: Expo SDK 57 (mobile/), web app (src/, Vite).
- New films in movieCatalog.js are seeded on server start (seedMoviesIfEmpty).
- Tests: server unit tests, mobile tests; simulations in server/scripts (simulateDaily.js,
  evaluateMatching.js); importers importWikidata.js, importPosters.js.
- Repo rule: regenerate the state index (`node scripts/state-index.mjs`) before pushing (CI checks).
- Builds: Expo free builds used up until 1 Dec; APKs built on GitHub Actions from branch
  claude/reelmates-apk-build (.github/workflows/reelmates-apk-github.yml).

7. MARKETING
------------
- Position: "Same 10 films for everyone, every day. What's your film personality?"
  Share hook: "I matched 82% with my friend on today's drop. Beat that." Keep dating out of
  the headline.
- Audience: film fans 18-30, strong in Kerala and South India; college film clubs, film
  Instagram pages, OTT discussion threads.
- Channels: daily results card on Instagram stories/WhatsApp status (with app name and friend
  code), small film meme/review pages (weekly "crowd verdict" graphic), college film clubs in
  Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, Bangalore, Chennai, Reddit (r/MalayalamMovies, r/kollywood,
  r/bollywood) "which film split the crowd" posts, challenge links.
- Targets (90 days): 1,500 installs, 30% keep a 3-day streak in week one, 0.3 card shares per
  daily player, reports answered within 24 h. Watch day-7 return; if under 1 in 5, fix the game
  before spending on acquisition.
- Competitors (Flickship, CineConnections, FilmMatch) look small and empty: proof that the dating
  pitch alone does not pull people in.
